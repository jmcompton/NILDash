Where to get the file

  1. LinkedIn → Settings & Privacy → Data privacy → Get a copy of your data.
  2. Pick Connections (not "the works" — that download is far larger and takes hours).
  3. LinkedIn emails a zip, usually within ten minutes. Unzip it and upload the Connections.csv inside.
