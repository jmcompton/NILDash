Your organization's IT admin needs to approve NILDash

Your Microsoft 365 account is set so that apps can only connect to company mailboxes after an administrator approves them. That is a setting your organization chose, not a problem with your account. It takes your admin about a minute, once, and then everyone in your organization can connect.

What NILDash is asking for: permission to send email as you, from your own mailbox, when you approve a pitch. It cannot read, change or delete your email.

  1. Copy this link and send it to your IT admin.
  2. They open it, sign in with their admin account, and click Accept.
  3. Come back here and click Connect again. It will go straight through.
