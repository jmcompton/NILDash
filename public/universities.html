Questions

What NIL offices ask us

Does NILDash represent athletes or negotiate deals?

No. NILDash is software for your NIL office. We find businesses and write the pitch. The business and the athlete agree on terms in your marketplace, and NILDash never takes a cut of what athletes are paid.

How do you handle compliance and NIL Go?

Every pitch asks for real work, like posts or an appearance. Businesses tied to your donors or boosters are held for your compliance office before anything is sent. For Division I deals of $600 or more, NILDash prepares the NIL Go report so the athlete can submit it on time.

Will this conflict with our sponsors?

You give us your sponsor list and exclusive categories, and NILDash skips those businesses. Pitches never offer your logos or uniforms. Every new business we bring in is passed to your media rights partner as a sponsor lead.

Do athletes need to sign up for anything?

No. We load your roster from your athletics site. Athletes are only contacted after they opt in through your office.

Whose email do the pitches come from?

Your NIL office. You connect your own Gmail or Outlook, and NILDash sends no more than 30 a day so your school's email stays in good standing. Nothing is sent without your approval.

How fast can we start?

Your roster is loaded within a day, and your first batch of deals is ready that week.
