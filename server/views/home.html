How a morning works

The work happens overnight. You get the time back.

  1. 01 · OVERNIGHT

    Overnight research

    While you're off the clock, NILDash looks at each athlete's school, sport and market, finds local businesses and brands that fit, finds the owner or marketing person, and verifies their email. Then it writes a pitch for each one.

  2. 02 · MORNING

    Morning approval

    You wake up to 5 deals per athlete on your Home screen. Each card shows the business, why it fits, who you are writing to and the pitch. Edit what you want, skip what you don't, and tap approve. That's your part.

  3. 03 · SENT

    Sent from your inbox

    Approved pitches go out from your own Gmail or Outlook, so replies land with you. When a business doesn't answer, the follow-up comes back to your morning to approve, so there's nothing to remember, and every reply is tracked on the deal. The rest of your day is yours.

Verified sending

Verified, or it doesn't send.

A bounced email hurts the inbox your name is on. So every pitch passes the same checks before it leaves.

  • ✓

    Every email is checked before it goes out

    The address is verified right before it sends. If it can't be verified, the email doesn't go, and the card offers a call or a DM instead.

  • ✓

    No guessed addresses

    If NILDash can't find and verify the right person's email, it doesn't write to them.

  • ✓

    One email per business contact every 4 days

    No contact gets more than one email from NILDash in any 4 days, counting every athlete and every agent.

  • ✓

    Unsubscribe and address on every pitch

    Every pitch carries an unsubscribe link and a postal address. An unsubscribe stops all mail from NILDash to that address.
