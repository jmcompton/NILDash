Questions

Is this cold email legal?

Yes. Business-to-business email is legal in the US when it's honest, identifies the sender, carries a business address, and honors unsubscribes. Every email we send does all four, and unsubscribes are handled automatically.

Where does the information come from?

Business listings, each business's public email records (DNS), and a verified email finder. We never claim to have scanned or tested anyone's systems.

What is an email security gap?

Most often a missing or monitor-only DMARC record, or a missing or broken SPF record. Either one lets a stranger send email that looks like it came from the business. It's a short fix for an IT company and a real risk for the business.

Will it hurt my email reputation?

We keep volumes low, send only to verified addresses, and spread sends through the day, the same way a careful salesperson would.

What if my area is taken?
